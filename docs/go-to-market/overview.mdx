A lending protocol does not get used because it works. It gets used when a Bitcoin holder trusts it with a real decision and when a lender is willing to fund that decision. This section explains how Writz plans to earn both, one step at a time.

<Note>
Writz is on testnet. This is a plan, not a record of results, and it contains no targets or forecasts. Steps advance when evidence says so, not on a calendar. The mainnet target stays [Q2 to Q3 2027](/products/writz-lend#mainnet), and it remains a target, not a commitment.
</Note>

## The strategy in one paragraph

Start narrow. Find Bitcoin holders who need dollars and do not want to sell, and show them, with guided sessions on testnet, exactly what a loan feels like, including what happens when the price falls. Build the lender side at the same time, starting with teams that already hold USDC on Stellar. Use the developer tools as a second door: every product built on Writz brings an audience we do not have to reach alone. Publish what would make a careful person trust the protocol before asking anyone to. Launch with low limits on borrowing, then raise them only after the protocol has behaved well in public.

## The market problem we are solving

Most Bitcoin holders who need cash today have three common options: sell and try to buy back later, hand their coins to a lender, or wrap them to use them in DeFi. Each one asks the holder to give something up. Writz offers a fourth option, where the Bitcoin stays on Bitcoin in a script tied to the borrower's own key.

That option has a real cost of its own. A borrower can be liquidated, and a new protocol has not yet earned trust. So the go-to-market job is less about announcing a new product and more about helping someone compare it honestly with what they do now.

## The principles behind the plan

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One customer before many" icon="bullseye">
    A team of four cannot run four audiences. Each side of the market starts with one group, and others wait until that group works.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Two sides, from the start" icon="scale-balanced">
    A loan needs a borrower and a lender. Neither side is treated as something that will follow on its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evidence before the next step" icon="clipboard-check">
    Every step has a signal to advance and a reason to stop. Claims about users come from real conversations and on-chain activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trust before reach" icon="shield-halved">
    We would rather be understood by a few people than be noticed by many. Risks are stated next to the claims they qualify.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Distribution never breaks the promise" icon="link-slash">
    No channel is worth wrapping the collateral or adding a custodian. Growth that needs either is growth we decline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use before incentives" icon="seedling">
    This plan does not depend on a token or on points. We want to learn whether people use Writz because it solves their problem.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
