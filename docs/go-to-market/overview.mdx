A lending protocol does not get used because it works. It gets used when a Bitcoin holder trusts it with a real decision and when a lender is willing to fund that decision. This section is the plan for earning both before mainnet.

<Note>
Writz is on testnet. The figures in this section are goals we hold ourselves to, not results. The mainnet target is [Q2 to Q3 2027](/products/writz-lend#mainnet), and it remains a target, not a commitment.
</Note>

## The goal

Reach the mainnet launch with:

- **10 Bitcoin holders** who have already completed a full loan on testnet: lock, borrow, repay, release.
- **3 lenders** who have given their conditions in writing.
- **1 institution** evaluating the protocol.

The launch cap is 50,000 USDC across the pool, with a planned 5,000 USDC per borrower. It is a ceiling, not something to fill on day one: the USDC comes from the pool, which lenders fill by depositing for the yield borrowers pay.

## The principles behind the plan

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Two sides, from the start" icon="scale-balanced">
    A loan needs a borrower and a lender. Neither side is treated as something that will follow on its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Liquidation first" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The first objection we heard from holders was liquidation, not custody. Every talk and every interview starts there, with numbers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every channel can be stopped" icon="clipboard-check">
    Each channel has an owner and a stated signal that ends it. Claims about users come from real conversations and on-chain activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Distribution never breaks the promise" icon="link-slash">
    No channel or agreement is worth wrapping the collateral or adding a custodian. Growth that needs either is growth we decline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No race for capacity" icon="gauge">
    We cap borrowing, not deposits, so nobody has to outbid anyone in miner fees to take part.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use before incentives" icon="seedling">
    This plan does not depend on a token or on points. We want to learn whether people use Writz because it solves their problem.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
