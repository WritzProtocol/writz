The number the team watches every week is one: **holders with a completed cycle on testnet, cumulative.** A completed cycle is lock, borrow, repay and release, the moment the promise is proven to the person who tried it.

## Goals by date

These are goals, not results. When a date passes, we report what happened against them.

## Review

- **Every Monday, from 9 November 2026:** what moved in each channel, and which channel stops.
- **The last Monday of every month:** a retrospective on the whole plan.

At every point we name the single stage where most people drop out, and that stage is where the team's effort goes. If people do not finish a loan, more reach only sends more people to the same problem.

## Rules for every figure we report

- **On-chain figures can be checked.** Wallets, transactions and loans come from public contract data, so anyone can verify them.
- **Testnet is labelled as testnet.** Activity with test coins shows that the system works. It is not demand for real loans.
- **Wallets are not people.** We report wallets and accounts, and we say so.
- **Agreements are stated as they are.** We say what was signed and at what stage, and we name an organization only with its consent.
- **Interviews are a signal, not a statistic.** We state how many people we spoke with, and we do not turn a few conversations into a percentage of the market.
- **Dates come with numbers.** Any figure we publish states when it was measured.
- **Interest is described, never promised.** We explain how the rate works and do not forecast returns.

## What would change this plan

A different first customer, a better channel or a lender design that does not work would each rewrite part of this section, and we will update it when that happens. The pages stay public, so that people can hold us to what we said and see why it changed.
