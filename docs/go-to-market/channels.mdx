A channel counts only if we can say who it reaches, what we tell them, what they do next and when we would stop. Posting more is not a strategy. We run a small number of channels at a time and add another only when one is working or has been stopped.

## In use now

These channels serve the [three fronts](/go-to-market/step-by-step) directly.

| Channel | Who it reaches | Next step we ask for | When we stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Direct conversations and guided sessions** | Bitcoin holders and USDC users, through people we know and communities we belong to | Take an interview, or try a full testnet loan with us | When new conversations stop teaching us anything |
| **Direct institutional outreach** | Treasuries, funds, custodians, and the public bodies behind sovereign and state reserves | A technical evaluation, then a testnet pilot | When a type of institution cannot use Writz without a custodian or wrapping |
| **Developer programs** (SDK, hackathons, bounties, [ideas page](/developers/request-for-startups)) | Stellar builders, starting in Latin America | Build something real on the tools, and keep it running | When projects stop as soon as the prize ends |
| **Founder-led writing** (technical posts, the whitepaper, Bitcoin and Stellar forums) | Researchers, builders and technical Bitcoin readers | Read the design, challenge it, try the tools | When it brings no one into the conversations above |

## What we are not doing

| We are not | Why |
|---|---|
| Buying reach, or paying for promotion | People who arrive this way are not the ones who need a loan, and trust cannot be rented |
| Running every social platform | Attention is limited, and we would rather do a few things well |
| Reporting followers as progress | A follower has not changed what they do. We measure the [next step](/go-to-market/measuring-progress) |
| Sending people into a flow that is not ready | A person who gets stuck rarely returns |
| Announcing partnerships we do not have | Interest or a letter of intent is not a partnership, and we only name partners with their agreement |

## Why one channel does not fit all

Each channel plays a different part in the same journey.

- **Writing and developer programs** build credibility, and they reach builders first. They rarely reach someone who will borrow next month.
- **Direct conversations** are slow, and they teach us the most.
- **Institutional outreach** is slow and has few but heavy outcomes.
- **Communities and partners** bring the people who would actually use a loan, and they only work once the product and its message are ready.

That is why the order matters. Sending people to a product before it is ready spends the one thing a new protocol cannot buy back, which is a first impression.
