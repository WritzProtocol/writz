Each channel has an owner, a cadence and a signal to stop it. We run a small number of channels at a time, and add another only when one is working or has been stopped.

## Channels

| Channel | For whom | Owner | What is done | Cadence | Stops if |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meetups, Bitcoin communities and Stellar chapters in Latin America** | Holders | Josue Araya | A 30-minute talk with the live flow, a sign-up list and interviews | 2 sessions a month | Fewer than 5 qualified holders after 3 sessions |
| **Guided sessions on testnet** | Holders | Kevin Membreño | One-to-one guidance through a full cycle, a price fall included | Every week | People complete it and do not ask to continue |
| **One-to-one conversations** | Lenders | Miguel Nieto | Explain where the interest comes from and what makes it lose; ask for conditions in writing | Every week | 0 of 5 give conditions |
| **Yield platforms** | Lenders | Miguel Nieto | Learn what they would require to offer the pool to their users | Within the 5 lender conversations | They decline, or require an audit before talking |
| **Direct outreach with a technical brief** | Institutions and reserves | The four cofounders | Map organizations and ask for their legal, custody, reporting and approval requirements | Every week | They require a custodian or wrapping the BTC: that deal is dropped |
| **X** | Everyone | Sebastián Salazar | One thread a week from the founders' accounts | Every week | Stops with the meetups channel |

Integrators are reached through the [developer tools](/developers/integrate), the [ideas page](/developers/request-for-startups), hackathons and bounties, outside this table.

## What we are not doing

| We are not | Why |
|---|---|
| Buying reach, or paying for promotion | People who arrive this way are not the ones who need a loan, and trust cannot be rented |
| Running every social platform | Attention is limited, and we would rather do a few things well |
| Reporting followers as progress | A follower has not changed what they do. We measure [completed cycles](/go-to-market/measuring-progress) |
| Sending people into a flow that is not ready | A person who gets stuck rarely returns |
| Announcing partnerships we do not have | Interest or a letter of intent is not a partnership, and we only name partners with their agreement |
