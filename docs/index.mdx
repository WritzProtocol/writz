Writz is a lending protocol on Stellar backed by native BTC. A Bitcoin Script holds the collateral on Bitcoin itself rather than a company, and it never becomes a wrapped token. Borrow USDC against it, repay on your own schedule, and recover your Bitcoin even if Writz disappears. Live on Stellar testnet.

<Warning>
Writz is on testnet only. The contracts are not audited yet. Never send real BTC, real USDC or a wallet that holds real funds to the testnet app.
</Warning>
